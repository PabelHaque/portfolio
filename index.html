Approach

How I work

Correctness is a mechanism, not a promise

The allocation engine refuses to run when its inputs are not in canonical order, because that ordering is what makes a re-run bit-identical. Weight columns must foot to exactly 1.000000. Constraints that matter get enforced in code, not documented in a README.

Access control fails closed

A scoped account with no branches assigned sees nothing and is told why — it does not quietly fall back to showing everything. Field staff see their own figures; each management tier sees exactly one level wider.

Humans keep the decisions

The agent factory has four approval gates that cannot be skipped, and silence is never treated as approval. Automation that quietly decides on your behalf is the failure mode I design against.

Cost is a design constraint

The study platform routes across three model providers, free and local tiers first, and will not make a paid API call unless explicitly switched on. Built for a context where the budget is real.
