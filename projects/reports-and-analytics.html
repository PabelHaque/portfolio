Reports & Analytics

Every month the MIS exports one file of up to 736,000 rows, every member and every loan. For years the organisation answered its questions from that file in Excel: the file froze machines, a single report took hours or a day, and a wrong cell was invisible. I automated the recurring reports in Python instead: 50+ regulator, finance, management and donor reports that now run in minutes from the month's export, each one footed to the source before it leaves.

The problem

The institution's MIS exports one monthly file, the CM Report: one row per member and loan, up to 736,000 rows and more than 80 columns, most of them headed in Bangla. It is the only complete picture of the loan book, and there was no reporting layer on top of it.

Everyone worked that file in Excel. At that size Excel opens slowly if at all, a pivot or lookup can freeze the machine, and every question meant hours or a day of filtering, lookups and copying between workbooks. The regulator's half-yearly return, finance's bank statement, portfolio-at-risk by branch and product, and the donors' sector splits were all assembled by hand every cycle, and a wrong cell stayed invisible until someone downstream noticed.

I moved the recurring reports to Python and its data libraries. The same file is read once, cleaned once, and each report runs in minutes with its totals reconciled to the source.

Before and after

The same questions, asked of the same file, before and after the notebooks. Each "after" runs in minutes on the month's export and foots its totals to the raw file before anything leaves.
