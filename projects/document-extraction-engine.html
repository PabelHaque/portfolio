Case study 08 · Document AI & identity

NID Extraction & KYC Reconciliation

A microfinance ERP holds identity data that was typed in by hand, years ago, from documents nobody kept. The member's national ID card is the actual source of truth. This reconciles the two, one branch or region at a time. It reads the barcode on the back of the card first, where the fields are exact, and treats text recognition as the fallback rather than the main path — because a missed field costs a review, and a wrong one silently matches the wrong member.

The problem

Know-your-customer data in the ERP was entered by branch staff over many years. Some of it is wrong. Finding out which parts means comparing it against the document it was copied from, for a book of members large enough that this can only happen in batches.

There is a structural difficulty underneath that. Identity documents carry no member ID, so the only way to connect a scanned card back to a member record is the national ID number itself. Which means the join key is also one of the fields being audited. If the ERP holds the wrong number for someone, a search by number will never find them — and those are precisely the worst records in the book, the ones most worth finding.

What I built

Four stages, run per scope, with a desktop interface over them.

Outcome

A batch of scanned cards becomes a reviewed list of concrete mismatches between what the ERP believes and what the member's document says, with the records whose ID number is wrong called out specifically. The correction step is built for and switched off, which is where it should stay until the review workflow has been used in anger. The fallback chain turned out to be a finding rather than a fallback. A member who can only be found by name and date of birth is a member whose ID number in the ERP is wrong, so the step that exists to rescue a failed lookup is also the detector for the exact records the project was commissioned to find.
